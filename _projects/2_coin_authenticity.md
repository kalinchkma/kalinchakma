A machine-learning web application that classifies ancient Bengal coins as **authentic or counterfeit**.

### Highlights

- Curated the dataset with a professor, collecting and cleaning coin images from multiple sources and auctions
- Trained and compared **ResNet-15**, **ResNet-152** and custom CNN models on Google Colab
- Wrapped the trained models in a web app for real-time predictions
- The dataset was published in _Data in Brief_ {% cite mamoon2024gupta %}

### Tech

`Python` · `PyTorch` · `ResNet` · `Pandas` · `NumPy`
